About EcoClean

Our mission to create a cleaner, greener world

Our Story

EcoClean was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: to prove that you don't need harsh chemicals to get a truly clean home. Our founder, a mother of two, was frustrated by the lack of truly safe, effective cleaning products on the market.

After years of research and development, we created a line of plant-based cleaning products that are tough on dirt but gentle on your family and the planet.

Our Values

  • Transparency - We list every ingredient
  • Sustainability - Refillable, recyclable packaging
  • Effectiveness - Tested against leading chemical brands
  • Compassion - Cruelty-free, never tested on animals

Our Commitment

Plant-Based Formulas

Every ingredient is derived from renewable plant sources. No petroleum, no phosphates, no synthetic fragrances.

Zero Waste Goals

Refill stations, concentrated formulas, and 100% recyclable packaging. We're working towards a circular economy.

Community Impact

1% of sales donated to environmental nonprofits. We partner with organizations cleaning our oceans and forests.
